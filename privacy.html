Privacy policy

Local Finance Parser · Updated October 20, 2026

This policy describes the intended personal-use workflow currently being configured by traviswei1325-ops. At this stage, Google account authorization has been implemented; the scheduled parsing and delivery workflow is still being built.

Information accessed and purpose

With the account owner's authorization, the program reads Gmail message contents, metadata and relevant attachments to identify financial documents and extract transaction dates, merchants, amounts, currencies, receipt items, future charges and document summaries. Gmail sending permission is used to deliver formatted bookkeeping results to the owner's account.

Processing and sharing

Email parsing is designed to run on the owner's Mac using a locally loaded AI model. The parser does not send email contents to a cloud AI model. Gmail remains hosted by Google, and formatted results are sent through Gmail and imported into the owner's Google Sheets by Google Apps Script.

The existing spreadsheet workflow is separate from the local parser and may send merchant information and ambiguous matching candidates to the OpenAI API for categorization and duplicate matching. This policy does not imply that all downstream spreadsheet processing is local.

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train generalized AI models. Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage and retention

Google authorization tokens are stored in macOS Keychain. The downloaded application connection file remains on the owner's Mac. The planned workflow stores processing checkpoints, extracted results and delivery records locally to support recovery and avoid duplicate delivery. These local records are retained until the owner deletes them; no automatic deletion schedule has been implemented yet. Gmail messages and spreadsheet records remain in the owner's Google account until deleted there.

Control and deletion

The owner can stop the local program and revoke its Google access at Google Account connections. Revoking access does not delete existing files, messages or spreadsheet rows. Local records and Keychain entries can be removed on the Mac; Google-held records can be deleted in Gmail and Google Sheets.

This informational website

This static website contains no sign-in form, advertising, analytics scripts or finance-data upload feature. GitHub Pages hosts it and may process standard request information under GitHub's privacy statement.

Contact and changes

Contact the maintainer through the project issue tracker. Do not include financial records, account tokens or other confidential information in public issues. This policy will be updated as the implementation changes.
